High-risk AI system

Under Article 6 your AI system is considered 'high-risk'. You will need to comply with specific obligations for high-risk systems depending on your entity type.

Provider obligations

As a provider of a high-risk AI system, you must comply with Article 16 obligations.

These include:

Ensuring your high-risk AI system meets all requirements in Section 2 before it is placed on the market or put into service.

Indicating your name, trade name or trademark, and contact address on the system, its packaging, or accompanying documentation.

Implementing a quality management system as outlined in Article 17.

Maintaining the required technical documentation according to Article 18.

Keeping system logs automatically generated by the AI system while under your control according to Article 19.

Completing the relevant conformity assessment procedure under Article 43.

Drawing up the EU declaration of conformity in line with Article 47.

Affixing the CE marking to the system or its packaging/documentation as required by Article 48.

Complying with registration obligations under Article 49(1).

Taking corrective actions and providing necessary information if the system is found non-compliant, as required by Article 20.

Demonstrating conformity with Section 2 requirements to competent authorities upon request.

Ensuring your high-risk AI system meets applicable accessibility requirements under Directives (EU) 2016/2102 and 2019/882.

AI Literacy obligations

You must take measures to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy for your staff (and other people dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf), taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training and the context the AI systems are to be used in, according to Article 4.
